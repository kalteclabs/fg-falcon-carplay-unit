### A CarPlay screen in your FG or FG MkII FALCON, in about 10 minutes. Pull out the tissue box, clip in the bracket, add any single-din head unit to your centre console!
**Jump to:** [Pick your power](#pick-your-power) · [Get started](#get-started) · [Kits](#kits) · [Licence](#licence)

---

<sub>IMAGE COMING SOON: the finished head unit fitted in the Falcon dash, CarPlay on screen.</sub>

The CarPlay head unit takes the place of the tissue box under your factory stereo. The factory stereo stays, and the sound plays through your Falcon's own speakers via the AUX. **no splicing required or touching any of your cars electricals!**

The parts cost about **A$65–75**, most of that being the head unit, and the bracket print file is **free** provided by KALTEC, the full install guide and the parts list are also all open source.

## GET STARTED

1. **[Install guide](install-guide.md):** step by step guide to build it yourself. pick how you'd like to power the unit: 

| | Cigarette lighter | Fuse box |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time** | About 10 minutes | About 30–45 minutes |
| **How** | Plug the unit into the cigarette lighter | Two fuse taps in the fuse box, cable hidden behind a panel |

3. **[Parts list](parts-list.md):** everything you need, with links and prices.
