The CarPlay head unit takes the place of the tissue box under your factory stereo. The factory stereo stays, and the sound plays through your Falcon's own speakers via the AUX. **No splicing or cutting into any of your car's wiring.**

The parts cost about **A$70**, head unit included (A$65–75 depending on how you power it). The bracket print file is **free** from KALTEC, and the full install guide and parts list are open source too.

It's fully reversible: keep the tissue box, and it goes straight back in. The bracket can also hold a second radio instead, if you're running aftermarket speakers or a subwoofer.

The bracket replaces your tissue box and holds any single-DIN head unit. No screws: it clips in with the two plastic clips off the tissue box. The print file is free, in three formats:

| Where | What you get | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GitHub** | STL, 3MF and STEP | Every file, including the STEP for changing the design |
| **MakerWorld** | Print profile with the settings below | Bambu Lab printers, print straight from Bambu Studio or Handy |
| **Printables** | STL | Any printer and slicer |

> **TODO (Aaron):** confirm the Printables listing is live and the link above is right, and paste the current MakerWorld and Printables descriptions so they can be matched to this copy.

**Printing it:** ASA or ABS recommended. PETG has been tested and works, but it handles heat less well, so use ASA or ABS if the car sits in the sun. PLA isn't suitable. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 5 top and bottom layers, 35% gyroid infill, no supports, front face down. About 3 hours and 73 g of ASA on a Bambu Lab H2S.

**What's in a kit:** a single-DIN CarPlay head unit (wired or wireless), already fitted in the KALTEC bracket (printed in ASA, with HE08 clips fitted, plus spares), an RCA-to-AUX lead, and the power lead (cigarette or fuse tap). A QR card in the box links to the [kit guide](kit-guide.md).

- **Wired or wireless:** both are available at launch. Wireless is A$20 more; nothing else differs.
- **The bracket:** black ASA, in a textured or carbon fibre finish. The carbon fibre texture comes from printing the first layer on a carbon fibre build plate.
- **Harnesses:** a power lead plus an RCA-to-AUX lead. They suit most aftermarket single-DIN head units, so if you already have a screen, the bracket plus a harness is all you need.
- **Shipping:** anywhere in Australia, A$21 standard or A$26 express. New Zealand, A$45 standard or A$65 express. Free pickup in Perth.
- **Returns:** no change-of-mind returns. If anything arrives faulty or not as described, email us and we'll sort it out.
