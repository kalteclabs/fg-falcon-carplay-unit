## PARTS

Building this yourself costs about **A$65–75**, and most of that price is the head unit itself (about $50).
Prices are in AUD. The links are the exact parts I used. Copy the **search term** into AliExpress for the current cheapest options.

### FUSE BOX LAYOUT

**If you're powering the unit via the cigarette port, you can skip this.** The steps for finding your fuses are in the [install guide](install-guide.md#75-pick-your-fuses-and-connect-the-taps).

Layouts are similar across FG models and years, but not identical. Always check your own car first: the layout is usually printed inside the fuse box lid, or check the owner's manual.

**If you can't find yours, I've made a basic layout that suits most FG Falcons.** The highlighted fuses are good ones to tap. On my FG, I used fuse **15** for BATTERY (always on) and fuse **21** for ACC (key on).

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### NO POWER

- **Both** BATTERY (yellow) and ACC (red) need power for the unit to turn on. Check both joins.
- Only **one** ground is needed: the wire your manual labels GND.
- Check the fuse on the back of the head unit.
- **Plug-in:** push the cig plug all the way in, and check the fuse in its tip (unscrew the ring). Test the outlet with a phone charger with the key on.
- **Fuse box:** check the tapped fuse is live with a meter, and that the ground bolt is tight.
- Unit turns on, then cuts out by itself? The blue **ANT** wire may be touching power. Tape it off on its own.

---

### LOSES THE CLOCK AND SETTINGS WHEN THE CAR TURNS OFF

- **Plug-in:** that's normal. BATTERY and ACC are joined, so the unit starts fresh each time. Use the [fuse box route](install-guide.md#7-fuse-box-power) if you want them kept.
- **Fuse box:** BATTERY (yellow) and ACC (red) are swapped, or both are on key-on fuses. BATTERY must go on an **always-on** fuse.

---

### NO SOUND

- The audio cable is in **MIC IN** instead of the RCA outs. Move it to **L-OUT / R-OUT** (red and white).
- The factory stereo isn't on AUX. Press <kbd>MEDIA</kbd> until it shows AUX.
- Check the cable is pushed fully into the AUX port, and turn up the volume on the head unit as well as the car.

---

### FLAT BATTERY, OR THE UNIT STAYS ON WITH THE KEY OUT

- **Fuse box:** ACC (red) is on an always-on fuse. Move it to a key-on fuse. Key off, the unit must switch off.
- **Plug-in:** if your cigarette lighter stays live with the key out, purchase a cig plug with an ON/OFF switch, or use the [fuse box route](install-guide.md#7-fuse-box-power).

### BRACKET IS LOOSE OR THE CLIPS WON'T SEAT

- Check both clips are pushed all the way onto the tabs. Cracked or worn clips? Replace them, they're cheap ([parts](#parts)).
- Wiring bunched up behind the unit can push the bracket out. Tuck it to the sides so nothing gets pinched.
- Push the bracket in evenly, both sides at once, until both clips click.
- Sometimes the console edges interfere with the bracket. Flex them out with your hands to seat it.

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kalteclabs/.github/main/fg-falcon-carplay-unit/install/console-slot-sides.png" alt="The plastic sides of the console slot highlighted: flex them out if the bracket catches" width="720"></p>

### HEAD UNIT IS TOO TIGHT OR TOO TALL FOR THE BRACKET

- Take off the metal sleeve if your unit came with one. It isn't used.
- Make sure you've printed the latest STL from the [free bracket page](free-bracket.md#download), at 100% scale.
- Lightly sand the ridges inside the bracket, evenly on both sides, until it presses in.
- Unit too **loose** instead? Add thin inserts or tape inside the bracket so it fits tight.
- Odd-sized unit? Adjust the [STEP file](free-bracket.md#fitment) to fit. Any single-DIN unit with the same power plug works.

---

### CARPLAY WON'T CONNECT

- **Wired:** use a good data cable (some cheap ones only charge), plugged into the unit's USB port.
- **Wireless:** Bluetooth and Wi-Fi both on. Forget the car on your phone and pair again.
- Check CarPlay is allowed on your phone: Settings → General → CarPlay.
- Restart the head unit: key off, wait 10 seconds, key on.

---

### NO RADIO ON THE HEAD UNIT

- The head unit needs its own [car radio antenna](https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005005732812892.html) plugged into the **ANT** socket on the back. The Falcon's antenna stays on the factory stereo. Search AliExpress for 'car radio antenna stick on' if yours didn't come with one.
- The blue **ANT** wire is not the antenna. Keep it taped off.
- Don't need it? The factory radio still works as normal.
