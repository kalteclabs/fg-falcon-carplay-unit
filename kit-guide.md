### Kit guide: fitting your KALTEC FG Falcon CarPlay kit
**FG, MkII and FG-X. Not FPV.**

This guide is for KALTEC kit owners: cigarette power or fuse tap power, with wired or wireless CarPlay. Everything is ready to plug in, so there's nothing to build. For more detail on any step, see the full [install guide](install-guide.md).

**Cigarette power:** about 10 minutes. **Fuse tap power:** about 30–45 minutes.

## 1. WHAT'S IN THE BOX

- Single-DIN CarPlay head unit (wired or wireless)
- KALTEC bracket, printed in ASA, with the plastic Ford interior clips already fitted (plus spares)
- RCA-to-AUX lead
- Power lead: cigarette power or fuse tap power

Check everything is there before you start. If anything is missing or damaged, see [help](#help).

## 2. TAKE OUT THE TISSUE BOX

No tools needed. Grip the bottom lip and pull it firmly towards you. It's held by two clips and pops straight out. Keep the tissue box: it goes straight back in if you ever remove the kit. [More detail](install-guide.md#1-take-out-the-tissue-box).

> **Video:** coming soon

## 3. CONNECT THE LEADS TO THE HEAD UNIT

Push the power lead's plug into the matching socket on the back of the head unit until it clicks. It only fits one way round. Plug the red and white ends of the RCA-to-AUX lead into the RCA outs marked **L-OUT / R-OUT**, not MIC IN. If your head unit came with a reverse camera or radio antenna, plug them in too. [More detail](install-guide.md#4-plug-in).

**Fuse tap power?** Do the fuse tap steps in [step 4](#4-power-cigarette-or-fuse-tap) first, so the lead is grounded and tapped in before the head unit goes in the slot.

## 4. POWER: CIGARETTE OR FUSE TAP

**Cigarette power:** plug the cigarette plug into the Falcon's cigarette socket, below the slot. That's it.

**Fuse tap power:** the lead's two power wires connect to the fuse box with two fuse taps, and the cable hides behind the console panel.

1. **Ground:** with the tissue box out, undo one of the two bolts inside the slot (M7), wrap the lead's ground wire under the washer and tighten it back down. Give it a tug to check it's tight. [More detail](install-guide.md#72-connect-the-ground).
2. **Find the fuse box:** take the key out and turn the lights off. The fuse box is under the dash in the driver's footwell, to the right of the steering column. Pull the cover off. [More detail](install-guide.md#73-find-the-fuse-box).
3. **Route the wires:** pop off the plastic panel on the driver's side of the centre console, below the slot. Run the two power wires down behind the panel and behind the steering column to the fuse box. Leave the panel off until you've tested in step 7. [More detail](install-guide.md#74-route-the-wires).
4. **Fit the taps:** the **ALWAYS ON** tap goes in an always-on fuse slot and the **KEY ON** tap in a key-on fuse slot. On our FG, we used fuse 15 for ALWAYS ON and fuse 21 for KEY ON, but check your own layout first. Never put KEY ON on an always-on fuse, and don't tap airbag (SRS), ABS, engine (PCM) or fuel pump fuses. [Picking your fuses and loading the taps](install-guide.md#75-pick-your-fuses-and-connect-the-taps).

## 5. PLUG THE AUX LEAD INTO THE FALCON

Plug the other end of the RCA-to-AUX lead into the Falcon's AUX input. Most Falcons have the AUX right next to the cigarette lighter. Push it all the way in.

> **Video:** coming soon

## 6. CLIP THE BRACKET INTO THE TISSUE BOX SLOT

Press the head unit into the bracket from the front until it sits flush. It's a tight press fit, so push firmly and evenly. Tuck the wiring behind the head unit so nothing gets pinched, line the bracket up with the slot, and push it in evenly, both sides at once, until both clips click. [More detail](install-guide.md#5-secure-it).

## 7. TURN ON AND SELECT AUX

Turn the key on and the head unit starts up. On the factory stereo, press <kbd>MEDIA</kbd> until the display shows AUX. **Fuse tap power:** turn the key off and check the unit switches off, then back on and check the clock and settings are still there. Then clip the console panel back on. [More detail](install-guide.md#6-test-it).

> **Video:** coming soon

## 8. CONNECT YOUR PHONE

**Wired CarPlay:** plug your phone into the head unit's USB port with a good data cable (some cheap cables only charge). **Wireless CarPlay:** turn on Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on your phone and pair it with the head unit once. Either way, check CarPlay is allowed on your phone: Settings → General → CarPlay.

## QUICK FIXES

**No sound**
- Check the RCA lead is in **L-OUT / R-OUT**, not MIC IN.
- Press <kbd>MEDIA</kbd> on the factory stereo until it shows AUX.
- Push the AUX plug fully in, and turn up the volume on the head unit as well as the car.

**No power**
- **Cigarette power:** push the plug all the way into the socket. Test the socket with a phone charger with the key on, and check the fuse in the plug's tip (unscrew the ring).
- **Fuse tap power:** check both taps are pushed fully in and the ground bolt is tight.
- Check the fuse on the back of the head unit.

**Phone won't connect**
- **Wired:** use a good data cable, plugged into the head unit's USB port.
- **Wireless:** Bluetooth and Wi-Fi both on. Forget the head unit on your phone and pair again.
- Restart the head unit: key off, wait 10 seconds, key on.

More fixes are in [troubleshooting](parts-list.md#troubleshooting).
